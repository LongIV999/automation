# Visual Philosophy: Nano Studio Professional (Brand Aligned)

## The Core Concept
The "Nano Studio" philosophy merges the creative freedom of AI with the precision of high-end photography. It visualizes "Nano Banana Pro" not just as software, but as a sleek, futuristic digital darkroom. The operational metaphor is "Magic Glass": interfaces that are transparent, light, and powerful, allowing the user to manipulate reality with a keystroke.

## Color and Material
Adhering to the **Anthropic Brand Guidelines**, the palette is sophisticated and grounded, avoiding the neon-chaos of typical cyberpunk in favor of "Premium Tech":

-   **Backgrounds**: **Dark (`#141413`)** provides a deep, cinematic void that makes the content pop.
-   **Primary Accents**: **Orange (`#d97757`)** is used for "Action" elements (cursors, active prompts, selection borders) – the "Banana" energy.
-   **Secondary Accents**: **Green (`#788c5d`)** represents the "Pro" camera focus rings, grid overlays, and success states.
-   **Light**: **Cream (`#faf9f5`)** is used for the primary typography and UI panels, offering high readability and warmth against the dark background.
-   **glass**: Frosted glass effects for UI panels to create depth layer.

## Typography
-   **Headings**: **Poppins** – Clean, geometric, and trustworthy. Used for punchy headlines like "Prompt Chuẩn = Ảnh Chất".
-   **Body Text**: **Lora** – Serif, editorial. It treats the "instructional" content like a high-end photography magazine article.

## Composition & Layout
-   **Split Screens**: Use the "Before/After" or "Real vs AI" layout frequently to demonstrate the tool's power.
-   **Overlay UI**: Superimpose "Holographic UI" elements (focus squares, rule-of-thirds grids, typing cursors) over the images to sell the "Pro Tool" vibe.
-   **Asymmetry**: Use asymmetric layouts where the text (instruction) balances the visual weight of the "Result Image".

## Visual Hierarchy
1.  **The Result**: The "Beautiful Photo" generated by AI is always the hero.
2.  **The Prompt**: The text prompt is treated as "Code" – monospaced or highlighted in Orange (`#d97757`).
3.  **The Instruction**: Clear, legible Lora text guiding the user.

## Image Style Guidance
-   **Mockups**: Show the "Nano Banana Pro" interface as a floating glass panel.
-   **Subjects**: Use subjects that demand high detail (macro nature, futuristic portraits) to show off the "Upscale" feature.
-   **Consistency**: Keep the "Robot with Banana" or "Cyber-Cat" motif as a recurring character to demonstrate consistency.
