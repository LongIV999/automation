# Queen Nail Bern - Content Creation Context

## Brand Identity

**Name:** Queen Nail Bern
**Industry:** Beauty & Wellness - Nail Salon
**Location:** Bern, Switzerland
**Language:** German (Deutsch)
**Target Audience:** Women 18-55 in Bern area, interested in beauty, self-care, nail art, fashion

## Brand Mission

Providing premium nail services with a focus on quality, hygiene, and the latest trends. We make every customer feel like royalty with perfect nails.

**Tagline:** "Perfektion für Ihre Nägel" (Perfection for your nails)

## Slide Structure Guidelines

### Slide 1: Title Slide (Hook)
- **Type:** title
- **Purpose:** Grab attention, introduce topic
- **Headline:** Bold, compelling statement about the topic (in German)
- **Subheadline:** Brief teaser (optional)
- **Visual:** Eye-catching nail design or related imagery

**Example:**
```
Headline: "5 Nageltrends für Winter 2026"
Subheadline: "Elegant & Winterlich"
Visual: "Beautiful winter-themed nail design with snowflake accents"
```

### Slides 2-5: Content Slides
- **Type:** content, list, or prompt
- **Purpose:** Deliver value, educate, inspire

**For Design Content:**
- Describe nail designs in detail
- Include color combinations
- Mention occasions (business, party, everyday)

**For Tips Content:**
- Actionable advice
- Step-by-step instructions
- Professional insights

**For Promotions:**
- Clear offer details
- Benefits highlighted
- Limited-time emphasis

### Slide 6: Engagement/Transition
- **Type:** content or list
- **Purpose:** Build connection, invite interaction
- Examples:
  - "Welcher Stil passt zu Ihnen?"
  - "Lassen Sie sich inspirieren"
  - "Buchen Sie Ihren Termin"

### Slide 7: CTA (Call to Action)
- **Type:** cta
- **Headline:** Strong call to action
- **Content:** Contact information, booking instructions
- **Visual:** Professional, inviting imagery

**Standard CTA Template:**
```
Headline: "Buchen Sie Ihren Termin"
Content: "Besuchen Sie uns in Bern für perfekte Nägel. Professionell, hygienisch, stilvoll."
Subheadline: "Nachricht senden für Terminvereinbarung"
Visual: "Queen Nail Bern salon interior or nail artist at work"
```

## Content Quality Standards

1. **Accuracy:** All nail care advice must be professional and correct
2. **Clarity:** Simple, clear German language
3. **Visual Appeal:** Describe beautiful, aspirational imagery
4. **Value:** Every slide should provide value to the reader
5. **Professionalism:** Maintain salon's premium image
6. **Call to Action:** Always end with clear next steps

---

## NO Emojis Policy

Do NOT use emojis in the content. Keep all text clean and professional.

❌ Wrong: "Schöne Nägel 💅✨"
✅ Right: "Schöne Nägel"

---

## Visual Descriptions

When writing "visual" field, describe what should be shown:

**Good Examples:**
- "Close-up of elegant French manicure with white tips"
- "Winter-themed nail art with snowflake designs and silver glitter"
- "Professional nail technician working on client's hands"
- "Before and after comparison of nail transformation"
- "Display of various nail polish colors in pink and nude tones"

**Avoid:**
- Generic descriptions
- Abstract concepts
- Text-only slides

---

## Brand Consistency

Every piece of content should:
- Reflect Queen Nail Bern's premium positioning
- Use proper German grammar and spelling
- Maintain elegant, professional tone
- Focus on customer benefits
- Include clear call to action
- Align with visual brand identity

## Slide Count: Exactly 7

All carousel content must have exactly 7 slides:
1. Title/Hook
2-6. Value Content
7. CTA

This structure ensures optimal engagement on Facebook while delivering complete value.
